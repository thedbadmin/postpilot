PostPilot Privacy Policy

Operated by TheDBAdmin (thedbadmin.com) · Last updated: 7 October 2026

PostPilot is a private tool that TheDBAdmin uses to manage its own social media accounts: it schedules posts on our LinkedIn profile, and it answers comments on our own Instagram account (@thedbadmin) by sending the link a person asked for. It is not offered to the public and is used only by our own team.

Instagram: what we access and why

PostPilot uses Instagram's official API, with permissions we granted for our own account. When you comment on one of our posts:

  • Your comment (its text, time and your Instagram username and ID) is read to check whether it contains a keyword we asked for, such as “GUIDE”.
  • If it does, we send you one direct message with the resource you asked for. Instagram only allows this within 7 days of your comment.
  • For some resources we first ask you to follow our account. In that case we read your replies to that message and check whether you follow our account, so we can send the link once you do.

We do not read your profile, your other messages, your followers or your posts. We do not message people who have not commented on our posts, and each person receives at most one link per campaign.

LinkedIn

PostPilot publishes posts that our team has written and approved to our own LinkedIn profile, using LinkedIn's official API. It does not read, collect or store other LinkedIn members' data, and it never likes, comments or messages on anyone's behalf.

What we store and where

  • For each answered Instagram comment: your username and Instagram ID, the comment text, which message we sent and when, and whether the link was delivered.
  • Our own posts, images and scheduling data.
  • Access tokens for our own accounts, kept as secrets.

This data is stored on computers we control. It is used only to run the features above and to avoid messaging the same person twice.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not send your Instagram data to AI services. (When our team uses PostPilot's AI writing assistant, only the topic or draft our team typed is sent to the AI provider.)

How long we keep it

Records of answered comments are kept for up to 12 months so the same person is not messaged twice, then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, send a direct message saying “delete my data” to @thedbadmin on Instagram, or use the contact options on thedbadmin.com. We delete every record linked to your Instagram account within 30 days and confirm by message.

You can also simply delete your comment. Instagram then no longer shows it to us.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @thedbadmin on Instagram or use the contact options on thedbadmin.com.
