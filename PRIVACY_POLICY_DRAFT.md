PostPilot is an internal tool operated by thedbadmin.com ("we") to write, schedule and publish posts on our own LinkedIn Page and on the LinkedIn profile of our own team member.

## What we access
- **LinkedIn account and Page authorisation.** When an authorised administrator signs in, LinkedIn gives PostPilot an access token. We use it only to publish posts the administrator has written and approved.
- **Content we create.** Post text, images and links that our team writes inside PostPilot.

## What we do not do
- We do not read, collect or store other LinkedIn members' personal data.
- We do not like, comment, message, follow or scrape on anyone's behalf.
- We do not sell, rent or share LinkedIn data with third parties.

## Where data is stored
Posts, images and access tokens are stored on infrastructure controlled by us. Access tokens are kept as secrets and are never shown in the interface or logs.

## AI writing assistance
When a team member asks for an AI draft, the topic or draft text is sent to the AI provider they configured (for example Groq, OpenAI or Anthropic) to generate the text. No LinkedIn account data is sent.

## Retention and deletion
Tokens expire after 60 days. Disconnecting the LinkedIn account in PostPilot deletes the stored token. On request, we delete stored posts and data.
